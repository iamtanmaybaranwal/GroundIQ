# On-Call Handbook

## Rotation
Each service has a primary and a secondary on-call engineer. The rotation is weekly and runs from Wednesday 10:00 to the following Wednesday 10:00, so that a handover never lands on a Monday morning or a Friday evening. No engineer is scheduled for more than one week in four.

## Compensation and time off
On-call weeks are compensated with an on-call allowance, and any engineer paged between 22:00 and 07:00 takes the following morning off without using leave. If a night is disrupted by more than two pages, the engineer takes the whole next day off.

## Paging policy
Only alerts that require immediate human action page a person. Everything else opens a ticket. An alert that pages more than twice in a rotation without a real customer impact must be tuned or deleted; alert fatigue is treated as a production defect and is reviewed in the weekly operational review.

## Handover
The outgoing on-call writes a handover note covering open incidents, ongoing risks, silenced alerts and anything deployed late in the week. The handover is a synchronous 15-minute conversation, not just a document.

## Expectations
On-call engineers must be able to reach a laptop and stable internet within 15 minutes of a page. If that is not possible for a period, arrange a swap in advance; there is no penalty for asking for a swap.
